# Tower — References & Inspiration

Links til 3D-printable tower designs, hydroponic guides, og relaterede projekter. Samlet til at lave informerede designvalg når v2 implementeres.

## 3D-printable hydroponic towers

*Tilføj links efterhånden som du finder dem under research-fase.*

- **3D Printed Hydroponic Tower Garden** (oprindelig inspiration nævnt af bruger) — find specifikt design og link
- **Printables.com** — søg "hydroponic tower", "vertical garden"
- **Thingiverse** — søg "vertical hydroponic"
- **GitHub** — søg "hydroponic" + "3d print"

## Hydroponic guides

- **Khan Academy / Wikipedia** for basis principper (NFT vs DWC vs drip)
- **r/hydro** og **r/Hydroponics** subreddits for praktiske erfaringer
- **General Hydroponics** Flora-serien dokumentation

## Akvarie-pumper (stille submersible)

- **Eheim CompactON-serien** — premium ultra-stille
- **Jebao DC-serien** — variabel hastighed, controllable
- **Generic AliExpress 5V/12V mini-pumper** — billig start

## Plante-specifikke guides

- Krydderurter (basilikum, mynte): https://nikolahalshydro.dk/ eller lignende DK-kilder
- Leafy greens hydroponic spacing og næringsstof-krav

## Smart home + Matter

- **Espressif ESP-Matter** dokumentation: https://github.com/espressif/esp-matter
- **Matter Specification 1.3+** (relevant for custom clusters)
- **CSA Connectivity Standards Alliance** ressourcer
